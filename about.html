About

Our Story

The best way to dress in your everyday life is to start with the essentials. We offer three basic T-shirt styles that are all about comfort, fit, and quality.

We started this store because we believe that everyone should have a great everyday T-shirt that holds up in the wash, so you never have to worry about shrinking. Some t-shirts shrink after a few washes, while others twist in the dryer, lose their collar, or simply become uncomfortable due to the thick fabric. We want you to have a shirt that you can wear every day without having to think about it.

We offer only three T-shirt styles on our website. We pay a specific focus to details and make our clothes comfortable and easy to wear. You can choose between three great options and be sure that the quality of any chosen piece will meet your expectations.

Our Mission

Our goal is to provide you with comfortable clothes that will keep you feeling great every day, but at an affordable price. We also strive to give you an easy ordering process with exceptional customer service.

Core Values

Focused 3-Shirt Lineup

We only produce three signature t-shirts. By narrowing the variety, no fabric is wasted on undesired styles, and our quality control is focused on three batches of shirts.

Made for Local Weather

Our shirts are made from soft cotton that will keep you comfortable throughout the day, without being constricting or excessively warm in a heated office place.

Personal Email Ordering

There is no need to navigate through payment or ordering systems. We personally receive each email and will contact you with details about the shipment of your chosen t-shirts.
